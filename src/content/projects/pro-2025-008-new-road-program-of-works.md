## Project overview

A new-road ("missing link") project under DPWH's Network Development Program: about 1.25 km of Portland cement concrete road from Sta. 1+509 to Sta. 2+759 on the Banaue–Barlig–Tanudan–Tabuk road in Banaue. The contract has two components:

| Component | Scope | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| CW1 | Construction of the concrete road: clearing, earthworks, subbase and 230 to 280 mm PCCP | Php 41,877,800.86 |
| CW2 | Drainage and slope protection: pipe culverts, box culvert, catch basins, flume, chutes and aprons, curb and gutter, stone masonry, cribwalls and double cribwalls | Php 21,685,979.14 |
| | **Approved budget for the contract** | **Php 63,563,780.00** |

## My specific responsibilities

- Prepared the program of works and budget cost, and the approved budget for the contract
- Prepared the bill of quantities and the detailed unit price analysis for each pay item
- Built the construction schedule bar chart

## Applicable standards

DPWH Standard Specifications for Highways, Bridges and Airports; DPWH forms DPWH-QMSP-13-10 to 13-16 for the program of works, approved budget and unit price analysis.
