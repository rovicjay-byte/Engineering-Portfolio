## Project overview

Plate 13 from the first-year Engineering Drawing and Plans course at Saint Mary's University, drawn entirely by hand.

## What the sheet shows

- **Footings:** plan and section of two footing–column types (F2-C2 and F3-C3), with vertical bars, ties or hoops, and bottom bars both ways
- **CHB walls:** plan and section of a 6-inch concrete hollow block wall with 10 mm bars at every third course both ways, on a Class A continuous footing with 12 mm longitudinal bars and 10 mm transverse bars at 0.30 m on centre
- **CHB firewall:** the same detail along a property line, showing the slab on grade over gravel and earth fill, and the finish lines

## Competencies demonstrated

Line weight, lettering and layout by hand, and reading and drawing reinforcement details. This is the foundation behind my later CAD and Civil 3D drawings.
