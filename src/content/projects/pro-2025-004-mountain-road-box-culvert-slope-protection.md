## Project overview

Construction of about 1.7 km of road through steep mountain terrain in Ifugao, under a DPWH program that builds access roads and bridges for communities across local government boundaries. The road is two 3.35 m Portland cement concrete lanes with concrete barriers. Cross drainage is a single-barrel 2.0 × 2.0 m reinforced concrete box culvert with a catch basin at the inlet and an outlet basin and flume carrying water down the slope. Cut and fill slopes are held by double cribwalls, reinforced concrete cantilever retaining walls and gabion walls, with weepholes and filter material behind the walls.

## Engineering context

The alignment runs along steep mountain terrain, so most of the road sits in side-hill cut and fill. That shapes almost every design decision: the road has to hold its grade on sharp curves with superelevation, water crossing the alignment needs a culvert and a flume to get it down the slope without eroding it, and both the cut slopes above and the fill slopes below need retaining structures. The wall type changes with height: double cribwalls for the tallest sections (the dimension table goes up to 14.5 m), reinforced concrete cantilever retaining walls, and gabion walls.

## From survey to plan set in Civil 3D

How the plan set was produced, and where each step shows up in the sheets below:

1. **Existing ground.** A surface built from the topographic survey of the corridor. It is the existing-ground line on every profile and cross section.
2. **Horizontal alignment.** Tangents and curves fitted to the terrain. The curve data are tabulated on the plan and profile sheets.
3. **Vertical profile.** Grades and vertical curves set against the existing ground, with superelevation on the curves. Shown in the profile and superelevation bands of the same sheets.
4. **Assembly and corridor.** The typical section (two 3.35 m concrete lanes, shoulders and barriers) applied along the alignment to build the finished-ground model.
5. **Sample lines and cross sections.** Sections cut at regular stations, including at the box culvert crossings. Shown on the four cross-section sheets.
6. **Earthwork.** Cut and fill end areas computed on each section and carried into the quantities.
7. **Structures and details.** Box culvert and flume, catch basin, double cribwall, retaining wall and gabion details drawn as typical sheets.
8. **Sheets.** Plan and profile, cross-section and detail sheets assembled into the approved plan set.

The Civil 3D model itself (surface, corridor and sample-line screenshots) is not yet published here.
