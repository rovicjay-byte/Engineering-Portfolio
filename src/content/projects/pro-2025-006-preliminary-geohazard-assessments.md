## Project overview

DPWH requires a preliminary geohazard assessment for each road project before detailed design. I prepared the reports for three FY 2026 projects on the Banaue–Mayoyao–Alfonso Lista–Isabela boundary road, a secondary national road through the mountains of Ifugao:

| Report | Project | Location | Project cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rehabilitation of roads with slips, slope collapse and landslide (slope protection) | Mayoyao | Php 28,445,000.00 |
| 2 | Off-carriageway improvement | Aguinaldo | Php 22,545,000.00 |
| 3 | Construction of drainage and slope protection structures | Alfonso Lista | Php 20,982,000.00 |

## What each report covers

- **Project description:** location, WGS84 and PRS92 coordinates, scope of work and cost
- **Geology:** regional geology, rock and soil type, geologic structures
- **Seismicity:** nearest active faults with their lengths and distances, historical earthquakes of magnitude 6 and above, and a deterministic seismic hazard analysis for the peak ground acceleration and seismic coefficients
- **Geomorphology, topography and climate**
- **Geohazard susceptibility:** volcanic, rain-induced and earthquake-induced landslide, flood, liquefaction, tsunami and storm surge, mining and karst
- **Problematic soils:** poorly compacted fills, contaminated, expansive and highly compressible soils
- **Assessment matrix:** each hazard rated on the DPWH 1 to 4 scale, from "no further action" to "specialist expertise required", with an overall classification for the site
- **Maps and photos:** 13 hazard maps of Ifugao with the site marked, and site photographs

## Findings

All three sites were rated **class 3 overall**: a hazard exists, and future survey and site investigation must identify and mitigate it. The deciding item in each case was unstable slopes. Liquefiable soils and volcanic hazard were rated 2 (can be handled within the project), and the remaining hazards 1. The nearest active fault is about 19.7 km from the Mayoyao site and 15.8 km from the other two, with uncorrected peak ground accelerations of 0.40 to 0.45 g.

## My specific responsibilities

- Prepared the three reports: the hazard screening, the discussions and the assessment matrix
- Prepared the hazard maps in QGIS
- The reports are signed by the Chief of the Planning and Design Section as the responsible officer

DPWH Guidelines on Geohazard Assessment, Volumes I–III (2017); DPWH Design Guidelines, Criteria and Standards (2015); National Structural Code of the Philippines (2015); hazard data from PHIVOLCS, the Mines and Geosciences Bureau and PAGASA.
