## Project overview

Reconstruction of three sections of a secondary road in the mountains of Ifugao where the road had been damaged by slips and slope collapse, about 270 m in all. On the cut side, stone masonry breastwalls up to 5 m high hold the slope above the road. On the valley side, the road edge is rebuilt with stone masonry walls, a cribwall and double cribwalls up to 8 m high. The carriageway gets Portland cement concrete pavement with widening and superelevation on curves, a 2.0 m shoulder with concrete curb and gutter, and reinforced concrete slope drains that carry runoff down the slope onto grouted riprap.

The plan is approved and the works are under procurement for C.Y. 2027.

## My specific responsibilities

- Design of the slip repairs: wall types, heights and limits along the alignment, and the roadway section
- Drafted the plan set in Civil 3D: plan and profile, cross sections and typical details
- Prepared the quantities
- Prepared the program of works and budget cost, approved budget for the contract, bill of quantities and detailed unit price analyses
- Built the construction schedule: bar chart with S-curve, a critical path method schedule and the precedence diagram network

## Applicable standards

DPWH Standard Specifications for Highways and Bridges (2013), including Item 405 (structural concrete) and Item 505 (stone masonry); DPWH pavement design and detailing standards.
