## Project overview

Two plates for Engineering Utilities 2, second year, Saint Mary's University, completed in July 2022. Both use the same single-storey house, about 9.5 m by 17 m, with a carport, veranda, kitchen, master bedroom with walk-in closet, two bedrooms and two toilets.

## Sheets prepared

- **Water distribution system:** the service line from the tap at the water main through a check valve, water meter and gate valve, then the supply runs to every fixture. Drawn in plan and in isometric, with a legend of valves, faucets, hose bibs, water closets, shower heads and fittings.
- **Sanitary layout:** floor drains, water closets, lavatories and the kitchen sink connected to the house drain and on to the septic tank, with cleanouts. Drawn in plan and in isometric, with its own legend.

## Competencies demonstrated

Laying out building water supply and drainage systems, and drawing them in isometric so the piping can be read in three dimensions.
