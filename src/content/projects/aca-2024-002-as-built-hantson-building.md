## Project overview

As-built documentation of the Hantson Building, an existing five-storey building at Saint Mary's University, prepared as fourth-year coursework.

## Sheets prepared

- Ground floor, second floor and typical third-to-fifth floor plans
- Front, rear and side elevations
- Schedule of columns with sizes, reinforcement and tie spacing

## Competencies demonstrated

Recording an existing structure accurately in drawings, and organizing structural information into schedules.
