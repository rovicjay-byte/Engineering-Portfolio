## Project overview

An individual architectural and structural plan set for a proposed three-storey apartment, prepared as fourth-year BS Civil Engineering coursework at Saint Mary's University.

## Sheets prepared

- **Architectural:** perspective and vicinity map; ground floor and typical second/third floor plans; front, rear and side elevations
- **Structural layouts:** foundation, column, slab, tie beam and beam layouts; schedules of structural members
- **Reinforced concrete details:** beam details by floor, column details, and one-way and two-way slab reinforcement details
- **Steel details:** column base plates with pedestals and anchor bolts; column-to-beam, beam-to-beam and column-to-column bolted and welded connections using wide-flange sections

## About the drawings shown

Selected sheets from the final set. Title blocks and the vicinity map are left out. The building is 30 m long and 10 m deep on a 9-by-5 column grid, with typical bays of 3.0 m and 4.5 m. The steel details use W18 wide-flange columns on concrete pedestals with 50 mm A325 anchor bolts, and bolted and welded beam and column connections.

## Competencies demonstrated

Structural layout and member scheduling, reinforced concrete detailing, and steel connection detailing.
