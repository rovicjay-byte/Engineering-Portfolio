## Project overview

Final exam plate for the second-year Computer-Aided Design course at Saint Mary's University, December 2021.

## What the sheet shows

A gate and pergola structure modeled in 3D, with paired columns, pergola beams and a tiled roof, shown from four viewpoints including an aerial view.

## Competencies demonstrated

Building a complete 3D model and presenting it clearly in perspective.
