## Project overview

An individual plan set for a proposed two-storey residential building, prepared as third-year BS Civil Engineering coursework at Saint Mary's University and completed in May 2023.

## Sheets prepared

- **Architectural:** perspective, site development plan and vicinity map; ground and second floor plans; front, rear and side elevations; sections; reflected ceiling plans; roof plan
- **Electrical:** riser diagram with general notes; lighting and switch layouts; power layouts
- **Plumbing:** septic tank details; water line layouts; sewer and drainage layouts, including roof drainage; water and sewer isometrics
- **Structural:** foundation plan; second floor and roof beam layouts; roof slab details with reinforcement notes

An earlier second-year computer-aided design exercise produced the floor plans for a separate two-storey house.

## Competencies demonstrated

Producing a coordinated multi-discipline drawing set, drafting standards, reinforcement detailing and building services layouts.

## About the drawings shown

Selected sheets from the final plates, May 2023. Title blocks, the vicinity map and the site development plan are left out because they show the address of a real property. Plans are drawn at 1:100. The 9 m wide house has a carport and porch, kitchen, dining and living areas, and a master bedroom with balconies on the second floor.
