## Project overview

Off-carriageway improvement of a tertiary road in Ifugao under DPWH's network development program. The work widens the Portland cement concrete pavement on curves with superelevation, adds concrete curb and gutter on both sides, and carries runoff away through reinforced concrete slope drains and box-type catch basins draining into HDPE pipe culverts with headwalls and aprons. Where the road sits on fill, the embankment is protected by stone masonry walls, sized from a table of wall heights checked against the allowable soil bearing pressure.

## My specific responsibilities

- Design work on the widening, drainage and embankment protection
- Drafted the plan set: plan and profile, cross sections and typical details
- Prepared the quantities
- Prepared the program of works, approved budget for the contract (Php 42.89 million), bill of quantities, detailed unit price analyses and the construction schedule

## Applicable standards

DPWH Standard Specifications for Highways, Bridges and Airports; DPWH pavement design and detailing standards.
